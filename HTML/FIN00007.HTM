TWA: AUSTIN IDOL OPENS THE SHOW
Allentown, Agricultural Hall | Segment 1 (4:00)

Austin Idol walks to the ring in a sequined robe to boos from most of the building
and screams from a few women in the front rows. He takes the house microphone from
the ring announcer and waits for the crowd to settle.

AUSTIN IDOL: Allentown, I know why you're here. You bought a ticket to see Austin
Idol and Bam Bam Bigelow finish what we started at McGonigle Hall. Twenty minutes.
For twenty minutes I had that man beat, and the bell saved him.

AUSTIN IDOL: So where is he tonight? I'll tell you where. Bam Bam Bigelow got on a
plane to Japan, because over there nobody asks him to get back in the ring with the
Universal Heartthrob. He ran. He ran halfway around the world.

The crowd chants for Bigelow. Idol smiles and lets it go on.

AUSTIN IDOL: Chant all you want. He can't hear you from Tokyo. And until he comes
home, there isn't a man in this territory who can stand in there with me for twenty
minutes.

Bob Armstrong's music hits and the building comes alive. Armstrong walks out with
Buddy Landell behind him and stops at ringside.

BOB ARMSTRONG: Austin, I've been listening to you run your mouth since Philadelphia.
You want somebody to stand in there with you? I'm not in Japan. I'm right here in
Allentown, and I've got my boots on.

AUSTIN IDOL: Bob, with all due respect, you were old when I was breaking into this
business.

BUDDY LANDELL: Then it ought to be easy for you. Tonight. Bob Armstrong and Austin
Idol. And I'll be right there in his corner, so nobody gets any ideas.

Idol looks at the crowd, then at Armstrong, and nods. He drops the microphone and
walks out of the ring as the crowd cheers the match being made.
